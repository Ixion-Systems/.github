## About the Organization

**Ixion Systems** is a space dedicated to the centralization, management, and showcasing of team-based software development.

Unlike projects started completely from scratch without a predefined direction, the main purpose of this organization is to **host and resolve projects that already have strict guidelines, clear requirements, and a specific problem to solve** (such as structured academic or course-based projects). 

It is important to emphasize that the solutions presented here **are not created entirely individually by me [@NicoWASD](https://github.com/NicoWASD), but are the result of teamwork and co-authorship alongside other members of this organization**, promoting collaborative development, joint version control, and the integration of different software architectures.

## Organization Projects

Here are the main repositories we collaborate on:

* **[InnovaLink](https://github.com/Ixion-Systems/InnovaLink):** A database-backed web platform designed to connect entrepreneurial talent with investors, facilitating structured networking in search of seed capital. *(Developed under the Waterfall methodology)*.
* **[Bob Do Something](https://github.com/Ixion-Systems/BOBDS_2.0_FrontEnd):** *(Work in progress)*. An advanced platform for centralized domestic robotics management. It automates operational routines using real-time telemetry and ultra-low latency control, freeing up users' time for what truly matters.
